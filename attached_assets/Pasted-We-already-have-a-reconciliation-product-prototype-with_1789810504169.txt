# SCREEN 1 — CLIENT RECONCILIATION KANBAN

The main reconciliation screen should be a Kanban board where each card represents ONE CLIENT, not an individual invoice.

The purpose of the board is to allow a finance user to immediately understand which clients are fully reconciled and which clients require attention.

Use these columns:

### 1. Fully Reconciled

Clients where all invoices that are due have been fully matched with received payments.

Example card:

Acme Technologies
US Client
Retainer • Monthly

Invoices: 6
Paid: $72,000
Outstanding: $0

Last payment:
Sep 12, 2026

Status:
✓ Fully reconciled

---

### 2. Partially Paid

Clients where payments have been received but the total amount received is less than the amount due.

Example:

TechNova Inc.
US Client
Retainer • Monthly

Invoices: 8
Invoiced: $96,000
Received: $82,000
Outstanding: $14,000

Status:
$14,000 outstanding

---

### 3. Payment Overdue

Clients with invoices that are due but no corresponding payment has been received.

Example:

ABC Solutions
India Client
Retainer • Quarterly

Invoice due:
Sep 10, 2026

Amount due:
₹4,50,000

Days overdue:
9

Status:
Payment overdue

---

### 4. Payment Received — Unmatched

Money has been received in the bank, but the system cannot confidently identify which invoice/client it belongs to.

Example:

Bank received:
$12,450

Reference:
WIRE-893421

Potential match:
3 invoices

Status:
Needs matching

---

### 5. Invoice Pending

The system expects an invoice for the client based on their recurring retainer/billing schedule, but an invoice has not yet been raised.

Example:

US Client
Retainer • Monthly

Expected invoice:
September 2026

Expected amount:
$8,000

Status:
Invoice not raised

---

### 6. Upcoming

Invoice has been raised but payment is not currently expected because the invoice is not yet due.

Example:

DataCore Inc.
US Client
Retainer • Monthly

Invoice:
INV-2088

Amount:
$15,000

Due:
Oct 5, 2026

Status:
Upcoming

---

### 7. Exception

Use this for reconciliation situations that need investigation and don't fit cleanly into the above categories.

Examples:

* Payment amount differs from invoice amount
* Multiple invoices potentially match one payment
* Payment appears to be short
* Duplicate payment
* Credit note affecting expected amount
* Currency/FX difference
* Unknown payment reference

Example:

CloudWorks Inc.

Invoice:
$20,000

Payment received:
$19,450

Difference:
$550

Status:
Reconciliation exception

Each client card should contain only the information needed to quickly understand its reconciliation state.

Show:

* Client name
* Country / client location
* Retainer frequency
* Number of active/open invoices
* Total invoiced amount
* Total received amount

* Most relevant status
* Last payment date

Cards should be compact enough that multiple clients can be viewed at once.

# IMPORTANT PRODUCT LOGIC

The client status should be derived from the underlying invoices and payments.

For example:

IF:

Total due invoices = total matched payments

→ Fully Reconciled

IF:

Total payments < total due invoices AND payment exists

→ Partially Paid

IF:

Invoice is due AND no payment exists

→ Payment Overdue

IF:

Bank payment exists AND no invoice is confidently matched

→ Payment Received — Unmatched

IF:

Expected billing period exists AND no invoice exists

→ Invoice Pending

IF:

Invoice exists AND due date is in the future

→ Upcoming

IF:

There is a discrepancy/ambiguous matching situation

→ Exception

Use the most important/current state when assigning a client to a Kanban column.
