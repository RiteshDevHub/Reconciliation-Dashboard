# ClearMatch

### AI-Powered Reconciliation Platform for IT Services Companies

ClearMatch is a reconciliation platform designed for IT services companies managing clients across India and the US. These companies often work on monthly or quarterly retainers, creating multiple invoices and payments across clients and bank accounts.

## The Problem

In a typical IT services company, the finance team has to manually compare invoices raised in accounting software with payments received across different bank accounts. With multiple invoices per client, recurring retainers, different currencies, partial payments, and unmatched transactions, it becomes difficult to quickly understand:

- Which clients are fully reconciled?
- Which invoices are still outstanding?
- Which payments cannot be matched to an invoice?
- How much money is still expected?
- Where are payment discrepancies occurring?

## The Solution

ClearMatch brings invoices and bank transactions into one reconciliation workspace.

The platform organizes clients through a reconciliation Kanban board based on their current financial status, allowing finance teams to quickly identify clients that need attention.

Users can open any client to see:

- All invoices raised
- Payments received
- Invoice-to-payment matching
- Outstanding amounts
- Unmatched payments
- Reconciliation discrepancies

The platform also allows users to generate reconciliation statements and invoices that can be shared directly with clients.

## Key Features

- **Client-level reconciliation Kanban**
- **Invoice and payment matching**
- **Outstanding payment calculations**
- **Unmatched payment identification**
- **Partial payment tracking**
- **Overdue invoice tracking**
- **Multi-client and multi-account reconciliation**
- **INR and USD transaction support**
- **Reconciliation statement generation**
- **Invoice generation**
- **Client sharing workflow**
- **Zoho Books integration**
- **Bank account integration prototype**
